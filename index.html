The Ecosystem

SpaceX

Founded in 2002, SpaceX's ultimate goal is to reduce space transportation costs to enable the colonization of Mars. With the reusable Falcon 9 and the massive Starship program, they are the dominant force in commercial spaceflight.

Tesla

Accelerating the world's transition to sustainable energy. Tesla isn't just an auto manufacturer; it's a software and energy company. From the Model S to the Cybertruck, and the Megapack, they redefine energy storage.

Neuralink

Building the future of brain-machine interfaces. The goal is to bridge the gap between human cognition and artificial intelligence, initially helping those with neurological conditions.

The Boring Company

Infrastructure and tunnel construction. By creating low-cost, fast-to-dig tunnel boring machines, the company aims to alleviate urban congestion with the Vegas Loop and future hyperloops.
